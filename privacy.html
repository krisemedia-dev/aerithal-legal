Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 24, 2026 · Aerithal for macOS (com.krisemedia.aerithal)
Data Not Collected

Zero personal data is collected, tracked, sold, or transmitted to Krise Media.

No Accounts or Ads

No sign-in, no analytics, no crash-reporting SDKs, and no advertisements.

100% Local Saves

Worlds, character skins, and settings stay inside your Mac's sandbox.

Same-Wi-Fi LAN Only

Optional co-op runs strictly over your private local network—no internet servers.

1. Data Collected by Krise Media

None. Keegan Porter / Krise Media does not collect any personal or usage data from Aerithal. In accordance with our embedded Apple Privacy Manifest (PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy):

  • Tracking: Disabled (NSPrivacyTracking = false).
  • Tracking Domains: None.
  • Collected Data Types: None (App Store Privacy Label: Data Not Collected).

Aerithal makes no outbound internet connections during solo or local co-op play.

2. Data Stored Locally on Your Mac

Aerithal saves your progress and preferences strictly inside your macOS App Sandbox container (~/Library/Containers/com.krisemedia.aerithal/Data/Library/Application Support/Aerithal/):

  • World Saves (IndexedDB — aerithal): World names, seeds, creation and last-played timestamps, time of day, boss progression, preview thumbnails, voxel block edits across all five realms, chest inventories, entities, and player positions/inventories.
  • Player Profile (localStorage): A locally generated random UUID, your chosen in-game display name, and your preset or custom character skin.
  • Settings (localStorage): Audio volume levels, view distance, FOV, mouse sensitivity, invert-Y preference, shader toggle, Wi-Fi Co-op toggle, mount camera preference, and custom keybindings.
Your Control: Delete any world from the Worlds screen by hovering over its card and holding the × button. Uninstalling Aerithal removes the app; removing its sandbox container deletes all local data. Krise Media has no access to your files.

3. Optional Same-Wi-Fi (LAN) Co-op Networking

Aerithal includes optional co-op play for up to 6 players strictly over your local Wi-Fi (LAN) network. Aerithal does not connect to internet matchmaking servers, public lobbies, or third-party WebRTC relays.

  • macOS Local Network Permission: macOS asks for Local Network access so friends on the same Wi-Fi can discover and join your world. Solo play works completely offline even if you decline this permission or disable Wi-Fi Co-op.
  • Hosting Discovery: When Wi-Fi Co-op is enabled in Settings or the Pause menu while hosting a world, Aerithal broadcasts small local-only UDP discovery packets (port 41234, multicast 239.255.42.99, and local subnet broadcast) containing the world name, your chosen display name, player count, app version/build, a random per-launch instance ID, and the host's local TCP port.
  • Joining & Private-Network Enforcement: On the Title and Join screens, Aerithal sends a local UDP probe to list active worlds on your Wi-Fi network and joins via direct local TCP. Aerithal enforces a strict private-IP filter (RFC 1918, link-local, and loopback addresses only) so co-op traffic never leaves your local network.
  • Shared Session Information: Players on your same Wi-Fi network can see your Mac's local LAN IP address, chosen display name, character skin, in-game actions, and visual waypoint pings. Aerithal has no text chat or voice chat.
  • Disabling Co-op: Toggle Wi-Fi Co-op off anytime in Settings or via the globe icon in the Pause menu to stop local discovery and close incoming connections immediately.

4. System APIs & Sandbox Permissions

  • Local Network (network.client / network.server): Used strictly for Same-Wi-Fi LAN discovery and co-op sessions.
  • Game Controllers: Optional Bluetooth and USB Xbox/PlayStation controller support via Apple's native GameController.framework.
  • Required-Reason System APIs: Standard macOS/Electron runtime APIs declared in PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy for local app preferences (UserDefaults, CA92.1), simulation timing (SystemBootTime, 35F9.1), file timestamps (FileTimestamp, C617.1), and storage availability (DiskSpace, E174.1). Never used for tracking or fingerprinting.
  • No Sensitive Hardware Access: Aerithal never requests camera, microphone, location, contacts, or photo library access.

5. Third-Party Services & Support Emails

Aerithal contains no third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, external CDN requests, or social sign-ins. All fonts, audio, artwork, and engine libraries are bundled locally inside the app.

If you voluntarily email us for customer support, we use your email address and message solely to respond to and resolve your inquiry, and you may request deletion of support correspondence at any time. Our static legal and support web pages include no analytics scripts, advertising cookies, or tracking forms (the hosting provider processes standard web requests to deliver the pages).

6. Children's Privacy

Aerithal does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your region). Because Aerithal has no user accounts, no internet servers, no chat features, and no data collection, families can play offline or locally with peace of mind, and may disable Wi-Fi Co-op in Settings for solo-only play.

7. Policy Updates & Contact

If future versions of Aerithal change how data or networking is handled, we will update this page and its Effective Date before releasing those changes.
