Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 25, 2026 · Aerithal for macOS (com.krisemedia.aerithal)
Data Not Collected

Zero personal data is collected, tracked, sold, or transmitted to Krise Media.

No Accounts or Ads

No sign-in, no analytics, no crash-reporting SDKs, and no advertisements.

100% Local Saves

Worlds, character skins, and settings stay inside your Mac's sandbox.

Encrypted P2P Co-op & Voice

Optional LAN & Join Code co-op plus live unrecorded P2P voice—no developer servers.

1. Data Collected by Krise Media

None. Keegan Porter / Krise Media does not collect any personal or usage data from Aerithal. In accordance with our embedded Apple Privacy Manifest (PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy):

  • Tracking: Disabled (NSPrivacyTracking = false).
  • Tracking Domains: None.
  • Collected Data Types: None (App Store Privacy Label: Data Not Collected).

Aerithal makes no outbound internet connections during solo play or Same-Wi-Fi (LAN) co-op. Optional Play-Anywhere remote co-op connects peer-to-peer solely to establish your session and never sends data to Krise Media.

2. Data Stored Locally on Your Mac

Aerithal saves your progress and preferences strictly inside your macOS App Sandbox container (~/Library/Containers/com.krisemedia.aerithal/Data/Library/Application Support/Aerithal/):

  • World Saves (IndexedDB — aerithal): World names, seeds, per-world 6-character Join Code metadata, creation and last-played timestamps, time of day, boss progression, preview thumbnails, voxel block edits across all five realms, chest inventories, entities, and player positions/inventories.
  • Player Profile (localStorage): A locally generated random UUID, your chosen in-game display name, and your preset or custom character skin.
  • Settings (localStorage): Audio volume levels (including Voice volume), view distance, FOV, mouse sensitivity, invert-Y preference, shader toggle, Co-op toggle, Voice Chat toggle, Push to Talk toggle, mount camera preference, and custom keybindings.
Your Control: Delete any world from the Worlds screen by hovering over its card and holding the × button. Uninstalling Aerithal removes the app; removing its sandbox container deletes all local data. Krise Media has no access to your files.

3. Optional Co-op Networking & In-Game Voice Chat

Aerithal includes optional peer-to-peer co-op play for up to 6 players over Same-Wi-Fi (LAN) and Play-Anywhere 6-Character Join Codes, plus optional live peer-to-peer In-Game Voice Chat. Solo play works 100% offline with zero network or microphone access.

Same-Wi-Fi (LAN) Co-op

  • macOS Local Network Permission: macOS asks for Local Network access (NSLocalNetworkUsageDescription) so friends on the same Wi-Fi can discover and join your world. Solo play works completely offline even if you decline this permission or disable Co-op.
  • Hosting Discovery & Direct Local TCP: When Co-op is enabled in Settings or the Pause menu while hosting a world, Aerithal broadcasts small local-only UDP discovery packets (port 41234, multicast 239.255.42.99, and local subnet broadcast) containing the world name, your chosen display name, player count, app version/build, a random per-launch instance ID, and the host's local TCP port. On the Title and Join screens, Aerithal probes port 41234 locally and connects via direct local TCP restricted to private LAN IP ranges (RFC 1918, link-local, and loopback).

Play-Anywhere Remote Co-op (6-Character Join Codes)

  • End-to-End Encrypted Ephemeral Signaling: When hosting or joining remotely with a 6-character Join Code, Aerithal exchanges end-to-end AES-256-GCM encrypted ephemeral signaling messages (with the signaling topic and encryption key derived locally from the 6-character code) over public signaling relays (ntfy.sh and public MQTT-over-WSS brokers) and public STUN servers (stun.l.google.com, stun.cloudflare.com) solely to establish a direct peer-to-peer WebRTC connection (DTLS-encrypted).
  • IP Address Visibility: During remote co-op, your public IP address is visible to the STUN servers, signaling relays, and the peers you connect with—standard to peer-to-peer WebRTC networking.
  • Zero Developer Servers or Logs: Keegan Porter / Krise Media operates no game servers, relays, or databases, and receives or stores no networking logs or session data.

Optional In-Game Voice Chat

  • Co-op Only (Never Accessed in Solo Play): Aerithal uses your Mac's microphone (NSMicrophoneUsageDescription / com.apple.security.device.audio-input) only when 2 or more players are in the same world and Voice Chat is enabled in Settings. Your microphone is never accessed during solo play.
  • Live Peer-to-Peer Audio — Never Recorded: Voice audio streams live peer-to-peer via encrypted WebRTC (DTLS-SRTP) and is never recorded, stored, logged, or routed through developer servers.
  • Player Controls & Listen-Only Mode: Mute or unmute anytime with U (or the Pause menu mic icon), switch to Push to Talk (T), adjust Voice volume, turn Voice Chat off in Settings, or decline microphone access to stay in automatic Listen-Only mode.

Shared Session Info & Disabling Co-op

  • What Peers See & Hear: Connected co-op players can see your chosen display name, character skin, in-game actions, visual waypoint pings, and—if Voice Chat is enabled—hear live peer-to-peer voice audio. Aerithal has no text chat.
  • Disabling Co-op: Toggle Co-op off anytime in Settings or via the globe icon in the Pause menu to stop local discovery, halt Join Code signaling, and close incoming connections immediately.

4. System APIs & Sandbox Permissions

  • Network (network.client / network.server / NSLocalNetworkUsageDescription): Used strictly for optional Same-Wi-Fi LAN discovery/co-op and optional Play-Anywhere peer-to-peer WebRTC co-op.
  • Microphone (com.apple.security.device.audio-input / NSMicrophoneUsageDescription): Used strictly for optional co-op voice chat when 2 or more players share a world and Voice Chat is enabled—never accessed in solo play.
  • Game Controllers: Optional Bluetooth and USB Xbox/PlayStation controller support via Apple's native GameController.framework.
  • Required-Reason System APIs: Standard macOS/Chromium runtime APIs declared in PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy for local app preferences (UserDefaults, CA92.1), simulation timing (SystemBootTime, 35F9.1), file timestamps (FileTimestamp, C617.1), and storage availability (DiskSpace, E174.1). Never used for tracking or fingerprinting.
  • No Camera, Location, Contacts, or Photos Access: Aerithal never requests or accesses your camera, location, contacts, or photo library.

5. Third-Party Services & Support Emails

Aerithal contains no third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, external CDN requests, or social sign-ins. All fonts, audio, artwork, and engine libraries are bundled locally inside the app. When you use optional Play-Anywhere Join Code co-op, Aerithal contacts standard public STUN servers and public signaling relays solely to broker encrypted ephemeral peer-to-peer WebRTC connections—no user accounts or tracking identifiers are sent.

If you voluntarily email us for customer support, we use your email address and message solely to respond to and resolve your inquiry, and you may request deletion of support correspondence at any time. Our static legal and support web pages include no analytics scripts, advertising cookies, or tracking forms (the hosting provider processes standard web requests to deliver the pages).

6. Children's Privacy

Aerithal does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your region). Because Aerithal has no user accounts, no developer servers, and no data collection, families can play offline or locally with peace of mind, and parents/guardians can disable Co-op and/or Voice Chat in Settings at any time for solo-only or silent play.

If future versions of Aerithal change how data or networking is handled, we will update this page and its Effective Date before releasing those changes.
