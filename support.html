Getting Started

  1. Character Setup: On first launch, choose your display name and pick a Preset hero or design a Custom voxel skin, then click the gold checkmark.
  2. Create a World: Click Play → click the + card → enter a world name (and optional seed) → click Create. New worlds spawn across 14 varied scenic overworld biomes.
  3. Craft & Progress: Mine wood and stone with Left Click, open Inventory & Crafting with E, place a Workbench with Right Click, and unlock seven crafting stations as you advance across five realms.
  4. Pause, Co-op & Auto-Save: Press Esc anytime to pause, copy your world's 6-character Join Code, toggle Co-op or mute your mic, open Settings, or exit safely. Worlds automatically save when pausing, returning to the menu, or closing the app.

Optional Requirements: Solo play and Same-Wi-Fi LAN co-op work completely offline. An internet connection is needed only for Play-Anywhere 6-Character Join Code co-op, and a microphone is needed only if you want to speak in In-Game Voice Chat (listen-only mode works automatically without a mic).

Co-op & Voice Chat Troubleshooting

Up to 6 friends (1 host + 5 guests) can play together with zero account setup—either automatically over the same Wi-Fi network or from anywhere using a 6-character Join Code.

Same-Wi-Fi (LAN) Co-op

  1. Check Wi-Fi Network: Make sure all Macs are on the exact same Wi-Fi network (avoid hotel/campus guest networks with Client Isolation enabled).
  2. Enable Co-op: On the host Mac, open Settings or the Pause menu and ensure Co-op (globe icon) is toggled on.
  3. Allow Local Network Access: Open System Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network and verify Aerithal is toggled on for all participating Macs.
  4. Check macOS Firewall: Under System Settings → Network → Firewall, ensure Aerithal is allowed to accept incoming connections.
  5. Match Versions: All players must run the same version and build of Aerithal. Update from the Mac App Store if you see a Version Mismatch or Update Required notice.

Play-Anywhere 6-Character Join Codes

  1. Copy Your Join Code (Host): While loaded inside your world with Co-op enabled, press Esc to open the Pause menu and click your world's 6-character Join Code badge to copy it. Share it only with people you trust.
  2. Join by Code (Guest): Open Join from the Title screen, paste or enter the 6-character Join Code, and connect.
  3. "World Offline" Notice: No active world was found for that code. Confirm the host is actively inside the world (not on the Title screen), has Co-op toggled on, is connected to the internet, and verify the 6 characters.
  4. "Connection Failed" Notice: Could not complete a direct peer-to-peer WebRTC connection across networks. Check your internet connection, temporarily disable strict VPNs or corporate/school firewalls that block UDP/WebRTC traffic, and confirm macOS Firewall allows Aerithal. On the same Wi-Fi network, use the automatic Same-Wi-Fi list instead.

In-Game Voice Chat

  1. Co-op Only Auto-Activation: Voice Chat activates automatically when 2 or more players are in the same world and Voice Chat is enabled in Settings (never accessed during solo play).
  2. Open Mic vs. Push to Talk: In Settings, toggle Voice Chat, adjust the Voice volume slider, or switch on Push to Talk (hold T to speak). Press U anytime (or click the mic icon in the Pause menu) to mute or unmute.
  3. macOS Microphone Permission: Open System Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone and confirm Aerithal is toggled on, then check your input device under System Settings → Sound → Input.
  4. Listen-Only Fallback: If you decline microphone permission or do not have a microphone connected, Aerithal switches seamlessly to Listen-Only mode so you can still hear friends in your world.

Gameplay, Performance & Save Data FAQ

  • How do I reach the next realm? Craft each boss's summoning relic at a Summoning Altar, summon and defeat the boss, and craft the next realm's Keystone from its dropped heart. Place the Keystone on open ground to raise a glowing Waystone Sanctuary Pool.
  • How do I ride Boats and Rocs? Right Click water with a crafted Wooden Boat to launch, Right Click to board, F to dismount, and F + Right Click to pick it back up. After defeating Tempest, Lord of the Roc in Skyreach, feed wild Rocs Roc Treats, equip a Saddle, and Right Click to fly.
  • Boosting Frame Rate: In Settings, reduce View distance (4–16) or switch Shaders off for smoother performance on battery power.
  • Where are my saves stored? Locally in your macOS App Sandbox at ~/Library/Containers/com.krisemedia.aerithal/Data/Library/Application Support/Aerithal/. Back up your Mac with Time Machine to protect worlds.
  • How do I delete a world? On the Worlds screen, hover over a world card and press and hold the × button until the red meter fills.
